# Third-party material

SERBench includes excerpts from upstream software repositories and their issue context. Their owners retain their rights. The `repo`, `source_path`, and available source metadata identify the original source; upstream repositories can be located at `https://github.com/{repo}`.

No blanket MIT or CC BY grant is made for upstream material. Consult the applicable upstream revision and license before reusing or redistributing excerpts. Source identifiers are attribution, not a substitute for applicable license obligations.

The release provenance manifest records the source archives and file hashes used to construct this interface. The repository intentionally excludes vendored model implementations and the supplementary archive's third-party dependency trees.
